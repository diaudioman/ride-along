Offline

Download for offline use

Downloads the app shell, curated place database, saved itinerary and source attribution. Browser-generated voices and Google Maps are separate and may require connectivity.

Airplane-mode test

After downloading, turn on airplane mode and reopen this app. A successful test should show your saved trip and curated place details. External Maps navigation will not work offline unless Maps itself has the area downloaded.

Sources & app status

Feature status

Implemented: worldwide planning/search, editable persistent trips, mapped stops, GPS approach narration, itinerary and nearby-story triggers, voice selection, repeat protection, factual story enrichment when available, Google Maps handoff, and local offline cache.

Needs real-phone verification: GPS accuracy, audio interruption, switching to Maps, installed-PWA behavior, storage limits and airplane-mode reopening.

Connectivity dependent: Google Maps directions, live closures, current web facts and uncached resources.

Not claimed: live traffic, paid Maps API routing, premium voice service, reliable background GPS/audio.

Help
